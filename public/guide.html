    Getting started

    This section is for everyone who uses T&C AI Academy. It covers signing in, activating your account, passwords, choosing a language, finding your way around, notifications, installing the academy on a phone, working offline and signing out. The academy's address is https://tc-ai-academy.onrender.com.

    Signing in

    1. Open the academy address in your browser. You see the Welcome Back page.
    2. In Employee ID or email, type your employee ID exactly as HR has it (for example TC-1042) or your work email.
    3. Type your Password.
    4. Select Sign in. You arrive on Home. If HR gave you a temporary password, the Choose a new password screen opens first (see below).

    A sign-in lasts a fixed 12 hours from the moment you sign in, even if you keep working. After that you are sent back to the sign-in page. A Super Admin can change the length with Session length (hours) in Settings. The new length applies to sign-ins made after the change.

    Tip. Capital letters matter in the employee ID. They do not matter in an email address.

    Signing in with your company account (single sign-on)

    If IT has connected company sign-in, the page shows a button above the form, for example Sign in with Microsoft or Sign in with your company account. Select it, sign in on your company's page, and you come back to Home. The password form stays available underneath (or sign in with your employee code and password).

    Single sign-on matches you by email address. Your company email must be the same as the email on your academy account, and the account must be active. Signing in this way never creates an account. HR must have added you first.

    Activating your account for the first time

    Anyone can open the sign-up page, but it does not create new accounts. It only lets you claim an account that HR has already created for you. You choose your own password there.

    1. On the sign-in page, next to First time here?, select Create your account (/register).
    2. Enter your Employee ID and your Work email. The email must be the one HR has on file for you.
    3. In Choose a password, type a password of at least 10 characters with at least one letter and one number. Type it again in Confirm password.
    4. Select Create your account. You are signed in and taken straight to Home.

    Activation works only when all of these are true:

    • HR has added you, and the employee ID and email match the record.
    • The account has never been signed in to.
    • HR added you on the People page, and nobody has given you a temporary password for the account yet. Accounts created by spreadsheet import, and accounts an administrator has given a temporary password with Change password, cannot be activated here. Sign in with the temporary password instead.
    • The account has not been deactivated.

    If any of these is not true, you always see the same message: We could not set up an account with those details. Check your employee ID and work email. If HR gave you a temporary password, or you have signed in before, use Sign in instead. Ask HR if the problem continues. The message is the same whatever the reason, so the page cannot be used to find out who works at the company. After 5 tries for the same employee ID within 15 minutes, you see Too many attempts. Please wait a few minutes and try again.

    Limits. Activation cannot reactivate a deactivated account. If you have signed in before, use Sign in or Forgot password? instead. People added through the HR sync cannot activate here. They sign in with single sign-on, or set a password with Forgot password?. When single sign-on is set up, activation is turned off and the form answers Your company signs in with single sign-on. Use the sign-in button on the sign-in page instead.
    For administrators. Activation only checks an employee ID and an email address, and a colleague could know both. Every successful activation is written to the audit log. So is every attempt refused because the ID or email did not match or the account could not be claimed (for example, it was already used or it has a temporary password). A Super Admin can review these in the Audit log. If someone added on the People page cannot activate their account, select Change password on their People record and give them the temporary password instead.

    Forgot your password

    1. On the sign-in page, select Forgot password?
    2. On Reset your password, enter your work Email and select Send reset link. You always see If that account exists, a reset link is on its way.
    3. Open the email and follow the link. The link works once, for one hour.
    4. On the New password page, type the new password in New password and again in Confirm password. Then select Change password.
    5. When you see Your password has been updated. You can sign in now., select Back to sign in.

    A reset also signs you out on every device and lifts any lockout. If you were waiting to replace a temporary password, you no longer need to. If the link has expired or was already used, you see This reset link is no longer valid. Please request a new one.

    Email must be switched on. Reset emails are only sent when a Super Admin has filled in SMTP email and ticked Enable email delivery in Settings. Until then the email is only written to the server log, and nobody receives it. Links go only to active accounts, not Invited or deactivated ones. If no email arrives, ask an administrator to set a temporary password for you (see below).

    Changing your password yourself

    1. Open Profile. Under Security, select Change password.
    2. Enter your Current password, then your New password twice, and select Change password.

    This screen has no menu and no Back link. If you change your mind, use your browser's Back button. The new password must be different from your current one. Changing your password here signs you out on all your other devices. You stay signed in on this one. Under Security you also see Recent sign-ins: your last 8 sign-in attempts, with the time, network address and OK or Failed.

    Temporary passwords and the forced password change

    How you get a temporary password depends on how HR added you:

    • Spreadsheet import: every new person gets a temporary password of their own. Right after the import, the administrator sees a Temporary passwords card that lists each new person's Employee ID, Full name and Temporary password, and can save the list with Download the list (CSV). The list is shown only once and is not saved anywhere. The administrator gives each person only their own password. People who were already in the academy keep their password. An imported account cannot be activated with Create your account. Sign in with your employee ID and the temporary password instead.
    • People page: the account has no password yet. You can activate it yourself with Create your account. Or an administrator can give you a temporary password with Change password, as below.
    • Change password on your People record: an administrator selects Change password under Actions. The new password appears once as A temporary password has been set: …, and the administrator gives it to you. This also signs you out everywhere and lifts any lockout. Only a Super Admin can do this for an administrator. After this, the account can no longer be activated with Create your account. If its Status is Invited, the administrator must also set it to Active, because an Invited account cannot sign in.
    1. Sign in with the temporary password.
    2. The Choose a new password screen opens. In Current password, type the temporary password.
    3. Enter a New password and repeat it in Confirm password. It must be at least 10 characters, with a letter and a number, and it must be different from the temporary password.
    4. Select Change password. You arrive on Home. You stay signed in on this device, and the account is signed out everywhere else.

    If the current password is wrong, the screen says That employee ID or password is not correct. If you type the temporary password again as the new one, it says Your new password must be different from your current one. Until you finish, every academy page sends you back to this screen. The screen has no Sign out button and no language selector.

    Single sign-on and temporary passwords. People added on the People page or by spreadsheet import still have to set their own password. After signing in with single sign-on, they are still sent to Choose a new password, and they need a temporary password from HR to get past it. Accounts created through the HR sync (SCIM) skip this step.

    Locked out after failed attempts

    After 5 wrong passwords, the account is locked for 15 minutes. While it is locked, even the right password is refused, and you see Too many attempts. Try again in N minutes. A Super Admin can change both numbers in Settings: Failed logins before lockout and Lockout duration (minutes).

    To get back in, you can:

    • wait until the lock ends;
    • reset your password with Forgot password?, which lifts the lock straight away;
    • sign in with single sign-on, if your company uses it, which also lifts the lock;
    • ask an administrator to select Change password on your People record. This sets a temporary password and lifts the lock. There is no separate Unlock button.
    Tip. The count of wrong passwords goes back to zero only after a successful sign-in or one of the resets above. When a lock simply runs out, one more wrong password locks the account again. If you are not sure of your password, reset it.

    Choosing your language

    The academy is available in English, العربية (Arabic) and Türkçe (Turkish). In Arabic the whole screen reads right to left, so the menu moves to the right-hand side.

    • Sign-in pages: use the globe selector at the top of the page.
    • Inside the academy on a computer: use the selector on the top bar, next to the bell.
    • Inside the academy on a phone: open the menu. The selector (EN, AR, TR) is near the bottom, above User guide and Sign out.
    • Profile: under Learning preferences, set Preferred language and select Save.

    How the academy decides which language to show

    • When you are signed in, it uses your account's preferred language. HR sets this when they add you, and you can change it later. If nobody chose one, it is English.
    • When you are not signed in, it uses the language last chosen on this device. The device remembers it for a year.
    • Otherwise it uses English.
    Tip. A language chosen on the sign-in page only applies on that device until you sign in. Once you sign in, your account's language takes over. To save a language to your account, choose it inside the academy or in Profile.

    Some notifications are written in your language when they are sent, and they stay in that language even if you change it later. Many others, such as certificate, badge and reminder messages, are always in English.

    The notifications bell

    The number on the bell is how many notifications you have not read yet. Above nine it shows "9+". Select the bell to open Notifications.

    The AI coach button

    The round chat button in the bottom corner (Open AI coach) opens the AI Learning Coach panel. It appears only when a Super Admin has ticked Enable AI features in the AI provider part of Settings.

    1. Select the chat button.
    2. Pick a suggested question, such as Explain this more simply. Or type your own in Ask about this lesson… and select Send.
    3. Optionally, change the answer style in the list at the top of the panel: Quick, Balanced (the default), In depth, Tutor me or Practise for a test.

    On a lesson page, the coach knows which lesson you are on. On other pages, it knows your job and department but not the page. It answers in your preferred language. The panel closes when you go to another page. An administrator may set a monthly AI budget that stops at its limit, for the company or for your department. If that budget is used up, the coach says so and stops answering until the next month. If the coach cannot answer, for example because the server has no AI key or you asked too many questions in a minute, it says The coach could not answer just now. Please try again. The coach can make mistakes, so check anything important.

    Notifications

    The Notifications page lists your 100 most recent notifications, newest first. Unread ones have a red bar along one edge. Each one shows a title, a short message, a category (Learning, Assessment, Certificate, Manager or System) and the date and time. If a notification has a related page, select it to go there.

    When you have unread notifications, a Mark all as read button appears. It also clears the number on the bell. If you have no notifications, the page says No notifications.

    Good to know. Opening a notification does not mark it as read. Only Mark all as read does that. While a notification is unread, a new one with exactly the same title is not added. For example, if "Certificate issued" is still unread, a second certificate does not add another notification. Use Mark all as read now and then, and check Certificates and My Learning directly. Only reminders can be emailed. A reminder is emailed only when it is set to email and email delivery is switched on.

    Installing the academy on your phone or computer

    The academy has no Install button of its own. Use your browser's install option instead. Once installed, it opens full screen from its own icon, like an app.

    • Android (Chrome): open the browser menu (⋮) and choose "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
    • iPhone or iPad (Safari): select Share, then "Add to Home Screen".
    • Computer (Chrome or Edge): select the install icon in the address bar, or choose Install from the browser menu.

    On Android, a long press on the icon offers shortcuts to My Learning, Training Sessions, Course Catalog and AI Skill Passport. Installing does not store your learning on the device. You still sign in, and pages still come from the server.

    Working with a weak or no connection

    The academy needs a connection for almost everything. To protect people who share a phone, pages are never stored on the device. If you open a page with no signal, you see the You are offline screen. This screen only appears on a device that has opened the academy at least once while connected. After anyone has signed out on that device, you may see a plain "Offline" message instead.

    What still works: lesson progress

    If a lesson is already open when the signal drops, you can still select Mark lesson complete. The lesson then shows Saved on this device — it will send when you are back online, and you stay on the same screen. When you leave the lesson, the time you spent on it is saved on the device the same way. A bar at the bottom of the screen shows what is happening:

    • Offline — your work is being saved on this device
    • Offline — N saved on this device, waiting to send
    • Sending N saved item(s)… when the connection is back

    Saved items are sent automatically when the connection returns. They are also sent the next time you open the academy while signed in as the same person. If the server keeps refusing an item (for example because the lesson was removed), the item is dropped after 10 tries.

    What does not work offline

    • Opening any other page, including the next lesson.
    • Assessments: answers cannot be saved or submitted.
    • File uploads and SCORM packages.
    • The AI coach, notifications and everything else.
    Do not sign out with work waiting. Signing out deletes anything saved on the device that has not been sent yet. Wait until the bar at the bottom disappears. Private or incognito windows may not be able to save work at all.
    The offline screen does not refresh itself. The screen says it closes when the connection returns, but it does not reload on its own. Once you have a signal, reload the page or reopen the app.

    Signing out

    1. On a phone, open the menu first.
    2. At the bottom of the menu, select Sign out. You return to the sign-in page.

    Signing out ends your session on this device. It also clears the academy files stored on the device, and any offline progress not yet sent. It does not sign you out on your other devices. Changing your password, a password reset, or an administrator's Change password, does. Closing the browser does not sign you out, because a sign-in lasts for the full session length (12 hours unless changed). Always sign out on a shared phone or computer.

    Learning day to day

    This section is for employees. Home and My Learning are in the main menu. Course Catalog and Training Sessions are under Explore.

    Your first visit: onboarding

    Onboarding is not forced. The Your next step card on Home always points to the next unfinished step.

    1. Tell us about your work (/onboarding). Department, Job title and Employee ID come from HR and are shown only; you cannot change them here. Answer the questions about AI experience, years of experience, weekly learning time (1, 2, 3 or 5 hours) and technical work. What are your main daily tasks? is optional. Select Save and continue.
    2. Complete your AI assessment (/onboarding/assessment). It takes about 20–25 minutes and answers save automatically. Select Start Assessment (or Resume assessment). When you submit, your results page opens with your level and a recommended path.
    3. Choose your goals. Home now shows Choose your AI goals. On /onboarding/goals, tick what you want AI to help with and select Start My Learning Path. Your recommendations are rebuilt from your goals and the results page opens again.
    4. Start your path. On the results page, review the recommended courses and select Start My Learning Path. The courses are added to My Learning, which then opens. Your weekly hours set your first plan and target date.

    The results page may also offer Optional: technical AI assessment (if you said your work is technical, or you scored 65% or more). Select Take the technical assessment or Not now.

    Note. The results page offers Start My Learning Path only while you have no courses at all. If you already have any course (for example one HR assigned, or one you enrolled in yourself), it shows Continue Learning instead and the recommended courses are not added. Enroll in them from the Course Catalog, or ask the L&D team to enroll you in the recommended path. If you start the path before choosing goals, you are not asked for goals again.

    • Your progress: Courses, Assessments, Current streak (days in a row on which you worked on a lesson; activity today or yesterday keeps it going) and Certificates.
    • Points and level: for example "Level 2 · Learner", your points, points to the next level, Recent badges (once you have any) and a See challenges link.

    A course page

    The course page (/learning/<id>) shows the description, Expected outcome, your progress and time spent, and a status: Not Started, In Progress, Pending Verification or Completed.

    Courses inside the academy

    Modules lists every lesson, with a tick when done. Select Start Course or Resume, or open any lesson. Lessons marked Optional do not count; only required lessons do. When all required lessons are done the course completes and you are notified. T&C internal courses also issue an academy certificate. A course from another provider whose lessons appear here earns a certificate only when someone confirms it: you are marked present at a classroom session for that course, or your manager signs it off. Ticking its lessons yourself (for example marking a video complete) earns none.

    Courses hosted by another provider

    1. Select Open Course and complete it in the new tab.
    2. Under Upload certificate or proof, choose your file (PDF, PNG or JPG, up to 8 MB) and add optional Notes.
    3. Select Upload certificate or proof. The course becomes Pending Verification.
    4. The L&D team checks it and you are notified.
    Upload proof. I have completed this course only sets Pending Verification; it never completes the course. L&D can verify only an uploaded file. Once L&D verifies your proof, the academy also issues its own certificate for the course.

    Supervisor sign-off

    Some practical courses need your manager to watch you do the work. At 100% the page shows Waiting for supervisor sign-off and the course appears in your manager's Practical sign-off list on their My Team page. Your manager is not sent a separate message, so tell them when you are ready. When they confirm, the course completes, its academy certificate is issued and you are notified. If no manager is recorded for you, nobody sees it; ask HR.

    Feedback

    Once a course is Completed, How was this course? appears. Rate How useful was this course? and How relevant was it to your work? from 1 to 5, answer Would you recommend it?, add an optional comment and select Submit. After that the page shows a thank-you instead of the form.

    Losing signal

    • Pages are not stored on your device. With no connection you see You are offline.

    Training Sessions

    /sessions groups sessions into My sessions, Upcoming and Past. Cards show the mode (In person, Online or Hybrid), date and time, location and room, instructor and places taken.

    1. Select Register. You see Your place is booked. If the session is full and has a waiting list, you join the list and see your position.

    Who sees the Technical assessment

    The Technical card appears only if one of these is true:

    • You answered yes to Does your work involve technical tasks (coding, data, systems)? during onboarding or on your Profile, or your job title is marked as technical.
    • Your latest placement score was 65% or more.
    • You have taken it before.

    The placement result page may also offer it, in a box called Optional: technical AI assessment, with Take the technical assessment and Not now. Not now takes you to My Learning.

    When the Final assessment unlocks

    The Final assessment opens when your learning path is at least 90% complete (standard setting). Progress is weighted by course hours, across all your courses that are not dropped. Until then the card says Available once you have completed 90% of your learning path.

    Module quizzes

    Quizzes are not listed on the Assessments page. Inside a course, a quiz lesson shows a Module quiz box with a Start quiz button. The quiz uses the same test screen as the other assessments. Things to know:

    • You do not need to pass the quiz to finish the lesson. You still click Mark lesson complete.
    • Start quiz starts a new attempt every time, even if you have already passed. Each attempt uses up one of your attempts.

    Attempts, waiting times and retries

    • An attempt counts only once you submit it. You can have only one open attempt per assessment. To come back to it later, click Resume assessment.
    • After your first attempt, the button changes to Review & Retry. Clicking it starts a new attempt straight away.
    • If a waiting time applies, the card says You can retry after and a date. Only the date is shown. For a one-hour wait that is today's date, so try again later.
    • When no attempts are left, the card says You have used all attempts for this assessment. Please contact your L&D team. L&D cannot reset attempts for one person. They can only raise the attempt limit for everyone.

    Taking an assessment

    1. Open Assessments. New employees can also start the placement from the onboarding page Your assessment is ready, with Start Assessment.
    2. On the card, click Start assessment. If you have an open attempt, click Resume assessment.
    3. Answer each question. Use Previous and Next to move between questions.
    4. On the last question, click Review answers.
    5. Check the summary, then click Submit assessment. Your result opens at once.

    The test screen

    • Progress. A progress bar and a line such as Question 3 of 20. The skill area being tested is shown above each question.
    • Question types. Select one answer, Select all that apply, or Write your answer (a text box). The standard assessments use only choice questions. Written questions appear only if L&D adds them to a question bank. If a written question has a marking guide, its criteria are listed under the box, headed Detailed scores.
    • Autosave. A choice is saved as soon as you click it. Written text is saved about a second after you stop typing. The top of the screen shows Saving… and then Saved. There is no separate save button. If you close the page and come back, you return to the question where your last answer was saved.
    • Save errors. If saving fails, you see We couldn't save your progress. Your previous progress is safe. Please try again. Only saved answers are scored, so change the answer again and wait for Saved before you submit.
    • Timer. The clock starts at the stated time plus 10 extra minutes. It keeps running even while the page is closed, and it turns red in the last two minutes.
    The timer does not submit for you. When it reaches 0:00, nothing happens. Your answers are kept, but the assessment is not submitted. You must still click Submit assessment.

    Review and submit

    The review screen is titled Submit your assessment? and tells you how many questions you answered. Unanswered questions score zero. If any are left, an Unanswered warning shows how many. Answered questions have a green tick, and unanswered ones show their number. Click any question to go back to it. Click Back to keep working, or Submit assessment to finish. You cannot undo a submission.

    How answers are scored

    • Single-choice questions score full marks or zero.
    • Select all that apply gives partial credit. Each correct choice adds marks and each wrong choice takes marks away. The score never goes below zero.
    • Written answers are scored automatically when you submit. The app looks for words that signal each criterion: Context, Clear objective, Data & constraints, Expected output and Verification / accuracy. Answers under 5 words score nothing. Answers under 12 words cannot earn full marks on any criterion.
    • Your overall percentage weights each skill area the assessment covers: AI Fundamentals 20%, Practical Workplace AI 25%, Prompting 25%, Responsible AI & Information Security 20%, Data & Automation Awareness 10%. The Technical assessment covers only Technical AI.
    Limits. Nobody reviews or changes the automatic score for a written answer, because the academy has no screen for grading written answers. You never see the written feedback the app produces. The result page also does not show which questions you got right or wrong.

    • Team members: one row per person, with Employee (name and job title, or employee code if no job title is set), Level (Not started if never assessed), Progress (how much of their course content is done, weighted by course length), Courses (done / total), Total learning hours and Last active (the last day the app recorded learning activity). Click View to open that person's page.

    A team member's page

    The page (/team/[userId]) shows the person's job title, department and employee code at the top, then the following sections.

    Progress and AI level

    • Current AI level, with Completion rate, Total learning hours and the number of valid Certificates. On this page, Completion rate means how much of their course content is done, weighted by course length. It is not the same calculation as the tile on My Team.
    • Detailed scores: competency scores from their latest AI assessment.
    • AI skill growth: a before-and-after comparison. It appears only once they have taken more than one assessment.
    • A list of their courses (headed My Learning), showing progress, status and any Expected completion date. Dropped courses are not listed.

    Skill matrix and ratings

    The Skill matrix compares what the person's job requires with what they hold. Skills that still have a gap are listed under Gaps to close, and skills that meet the requirement are under Met. Each row shows held / required and where the rating came from (Self-rated, Observed by manager, or Not rated yet), with Rated by and a name where known. Critical skills that still have a gap carry a Critical tag. The Job readiness percentage (requirements met out of all requirements) is shown beside the job title.

    To record what you have seen:

    1. Find the skill in the matrix.
    2. Choose a level from the list on that row: 0 None, 1 Aware, 2 With help, 3 Independent, 4 Proficient, 5 Can teach it.
    3. The rating saves as soon as you choose it. There is no Save button. If the save fails, the list goes back to the stored level and shows the reason.
    • Your rating is recorded as Observed by manager with your name, and it is written to the audit log.
    • Your rating replaces an earlier self-rating. If the employee rates themselves later, their self-rating does not replace yours.
    • If your rating reaches the target of an open development goal (proposed or approved), that goal closes as Done. A self-rating never closes a goal.
    Limits. The rating list has no comment box. The only place to record a written observation is Practical sign-off (below). The labels From assessment and From completed learning exist, but the app does not currently record ratings from those sources, so skill levels come only from self-ratings and managers. If the page says No job title is set, so there is nothing to measure against. or This job title has no skill requirements yet., there is nothing to rate. Job skill requirements come from configuration, and there is no admin screen for editing them.

    Development plan

    To build a plan:

    1. Click Draft a plan from the gaps. The button appears only when the matrix shows gaps. It proposes a goal for every critical gap and for up to three of the largest other gaps. Skills that already have an open goal are skipped. If goals were added you will see Plan drafted. Nothing is committed until you approve it., otherwise just Saved.
    2. Each goal shows the skill, the level range (for example 1 → 3) and its status: Proposed, Approved or Done. Under each goal are up to three courses that close the gap. Clicking one opens the Course Catalog searched for that course. If no course fits, the goal says No course in the catalogue covers this yet.
    3. Pick a Target date if you want one, then click Approve. The employee gets the notification Development goal agreed: {skill}.
    4. Click Drop to remove a goal from the plan. The employee is not notified. If the gap is still one of the largest next time you click Draft a plan from the gaps, the goal is proposed again.

    The employee sees both proposed and approved goals on their Skills page.

    Changing a date. There is no separate Save for dates. To change the target date of an approved goal, pick the new date and click Approve again. The employee may get the agreed notification again. Leaving the date empty keeps the existing one, so a date cannot be cleared once set.

    Proposals from the employee

    On My career, an employee can click Ask my manager to plan for this for a next role on their career ladder. This adds every skill gap for that role to their plan as a Proposed goal. Skills that already have a goal in any state, including ones you dropped, are left alone. If you are their recorded manager, you get the notification {name} asked to plan for {role}, which opens their page. Approve or drop these goals the same way as any others. On the plan they look the same as the ones you drafted, and the role they came from is not shown.

    A skill that the next role needs but the current job does not is not in the Skill matrix, so you cannot rate it there. Such a goal closes only if a practical sign-off records that skill at its target level. Otherwise, drop it when you agree it is met.

    Next roles

    If the person's job sits on a career ladder, the Next roles card shows each next role with {percent}% ready · Gaps: {count}. If that role has no requirements, it shows No skill requirements set for this role yet instead. The card is read-only. Career ladders come from configuration.

    Recommend a course

    1. In Recommend a course, type at least two letters in Course and pick a course from the list. Only published, available courses appear. Courses the person is enrolled in or has completed are hidden.
    2. Set Expected completion in days, between 7 and 365. The default is 30.
    3. Click Nominate for learning.

    The course is added to their learning as an assigned course with that due date. They get a notification saying you recommended a course, and the action is written to the audit log.

    • There is no way to withdraw or change a nomination from this page.
    • A course the person dropped earlier still shows in the list, but nominating it fails with Enrolled.

    Development goals (free text)

    The Development goals card (Set development goals) holds a free-text note of up to 2,000 characters. Click Save to keep it.

    Who sees this note. Only you and administrators who open this page can see it. The employee does not see it anywhere in the app, and the course-suggestion engine does not read what it says. Use the Development plan for goals the employee should see.
    Administrators. An administrator with users.view can open any employee's page, for example from Succession. For anyone who is not their own direct report, the skill matrix is read-only and there is no development plan. The Recommend a course and Development goals forms still appear, but saving them returns You don't have access to this area.

    Practical sign-off

    An administrator can mark a course Needs supervisor sign-off. For these courses, finishing the content does not complete the course. It stays open until the employee's manager confirms they watched the work being done. Meanwhile the employee sees Waiting for supervisor sign-off on their course page.

    A person appears on /team/sign-off when all of these are true:

    • they are your direct report,
    • the course needs sign-off,
    • their progress is 100%,
    • nobody has signed it off yet,
    • the course is neither completed nor dropped.

    To sign off:

    1. On My Team, click Practical sign-off (n).
    2. Find the card for the person and course. Fill in What you observed: what they did, and how well. This is required, needs 10 to 1,000 characters and is kept on their record.
    3. If the course is linked to skills, you can choose the Skill demonstrated, then a level under Rate (the default is 3). This is optional. It records an Observed by manager rating, with your observation as its note.
    4. Click Confirm sign-off.

    The employee is notified (Signed off: {course}), and the course is marked completed straight away. Completion works as it does everywhere else: the employee gets a course-complete notification, a certificate is issued and badges are checked. Your sign-off counts as evidence, so a course from outside the academy also earns a certificate once you sign it off.

    There are two exceptions:

    • If the employee also uploaded proof that an administrator has not yet checked, the course completes only after that check.
    • When recurring training assigns the course again, the old sign-off is cleared, so you need to watch and sign off again.
    Limits. You are not notified when a sign-off is waiting, so look for the button on My Team. Only the employee's recorded manager can sign off, and administrators cannot do it for you. If an employee has no manager on record, nobody can sign them off until HR sets one.

    Waiting for your review

    When a team member submits a Workplace challenge, you get the notification A workplace challenge needs your review. The queue at /team/reviews lists open submissions with the oldest first. Each one shows the title, the employee, their code, the submission date, its status and their answers: Business problem, Current process, Where AI can help, Prompt / workflow, Expected output, Risks, How you will check the output and Estimated benefit.

    1. Under Status, choose Approved or Needs some work.
    2. You can enter a score in the Score field. This is optional, and the field label shows the maximum.
    3. Write Reviewer feedback. This is required.
    4. Click Submit. The form changes to Saved.

    The employee sees your full feedback, and the score if you gave one, on their workplace challenge page.

    • Approved: the employee is told the challenge was approved, along with your feedback. The idea is added to the AI opportunities pipeline under Administration → AI opportunities, with the status Identified and the employee as owner. Badges are checked, and the programme certificate is issued automatically if the employee now meets every programme requirement. The approval counts towards the certificate. The score does not.
    • Needs some work: the employee is notified with your feedback. They can edit the challenge and submit it again. It then returns to your queue and you are notified again.
    Note. You cannot change a decision after you submit it. If the employee has no manager on record when they submit, nobody is notified. Administrators with enrollments.manage see every open workplace challenge in the company when they open /team/reviews, including those from employees with no manager. The button on My Team counts only your own team.

    The team view of the Learning calendar

    Open Calendar and choose the My team tab (/calendar?scope=team). Mine switches back to your own items. The tabs appear only if you have team.view and at least one direct report. The team view lists your reports' items for the next 90 days, plus anything overdue however old, grouped by month. Each item shows the person's name, and clicking it opens their team member page.

    • Learning deadline: due dates of courses that are not finished.
    • Classroom session: scheduled sessions they are registered or waitlisted for.
    • Certificate expires: certificates with an expiry date. The app does not set expiry dates itself, so this is usually empty.
    • Development goal: approved goals that have a target date. Proposed goals do not appear.

    Late items carry an Overdue tag. If nothing is due, the page shows Nothing is due in the next 90 days.

    A good monthly routine

    • Open My Team. Clear any Practical sign-off and Waiting for your review items. You are not notified about sign-offs, so the button is your only signal.
    • Check the Overdue learning tile and the Last active column, and talk to anyone who has stalled.
    • Open Calendar → My team and look ahead at deadlines, sessions and goal dates.
    • Check your notifications for {name} asked to plan for {role}, then approve or drop those proposed goals.
    • Update skill ratings where you have seen a change. Goals close by themselves when your rating reaches their target.
    • For anyone without a plan, click Draft a plan from the gaps. Approve the goals with target dates, and use Nominate for learning for the courses that close them.

    A person's record

    Click Edit on a row. Below the form, the record shows Assessment history, the person's enrolments (under My Learning), a count of their valid certificates and Recent sign-ins. The Actions card has three buttons:

    • Change password creates a temporary password and shows it once. It also unlocks the account and signs the person out everywhere. They must choose a new password the next time they sign in. The new password must be different from the temporary one, and choosing it signs them out on every other device or browser.
    • Run recommendation rebuilds the person's suggested learning and notifies them.
    • The third button is labelled with the status it will switch to: Inactive (red) or Active. It acts straight away, without asking you to confirm. Deactivating also signs the person out at once.

It must contain their name as HR holds it (they may add middle names), in Latin or Arabic letters, and it is then locked. If it needs correcting, type the name here (3 to 80 letters, all Latin or all Arabic) and click Save. A name you save here counts as confirmed, so the person can download straight away. To ask the person to confirm their name again, clear the field and save. Changing Full name does not change a name already set here.

The table shows only the first 200 rows, but every row is imported.
    4. Click Confirm — N. The result reads "X created · Y updated · Z skipped".
    5. If new people were created, a Temporary passwords card appears below. It lists each new person's Employee ID, Full name and Temporary password. Click Download the list (CSV) to save it as a spreadsheet with the columns Employee ID, Full name, Email and Temporary password. The list is shown only once and is not saved anywhere, so download it before you leave the page.

They sign in with their Employee ID and that password, and must choose a new password at first sign-in. Imported people cannot claim their accounts at /register.     Enrolments

    To assign a course to a group

    1. On /admin/enrollments, go to Nominate for learning. Type two or more letters in Course and pick a published course.
    2. In Assign to, choose Everyone, Department, Job family, Job title, Location or Shift. Then pick the group in Which one.
    3. Set Expected completion (days). The default is 30.
    4. Set Type to Assigned or Mandatory. This label is recorded on each enrolment.
    5. Click Apply. The result reads "N employees assigned".

    Only Active people are enrolled, and each one is notified. Anyone already enrolled in the course is skipped, even if they completed it. This form has no option to assign a course to one named person. There is also no way to remove a single enrolment or change one person's due date.

    Shift. Nothing in the app sets a shift: not the People form, the import file or HR sync. So the Shift list is empty unless shifts were written to the database directly.

    Recurring training

    Under Recurring training, pick the course and the group. Set Repeat every (months) (default 12), Expected completion (days) and Type (default Mandatory). Click Save. Nothing is assigned at that moment. The first assignments happen at the scheduler's next daily run, within 24 hours.

    Each day, the scheduler does two things for active rules:

    • It enrols every Active person in the group who does not have the course yet, including new joiners.
    • It re-enrols anyone whose last completion is older than the repeat period, counted in calendar months. Re-enrolling resets their progress and any supervisor sign-off, but keeps the old certificate. Completing the course again earns a new certificate.

    Rules for unpublished courses are skipped.

    Under Recurring rules, each rule shows when it last ran (or Never run), and has a Pause or Resume button. Rules cannot be edited or deleted. To change a rule, pause it and create a new one.

    To verify an external proof

    Proofs uploaded by learners wait under Upload certificate or proof. The Waiting for verification figure at the top shows how many are waiting.

    1. Open the file by clicking its name on the card.
    2. Choose Verified or Needs another look. If you like, add Reviewer feedback.
    3. Click Submit.

    Verified marks the course Completed, issues its certificate, awards any badges earned and notifies the learner. A course that needs supervisor sign-off waits at 100% until the sign-off is given. The sign-off then completes it and issues the certificate. Needs another look returns the enrolment to In progress and notifies the learner with your feedback.

    A proof can be decided only once. If someone else has already decided it, you see "Someone has already reviewed this proof. Reload to see the decision."

    1. Enter the Employee ID, Course, Completion date and hours.
    2. If you like, add the Provider, Certificate URL and Notes.
    3. Click Add.

    Training sessions

    To create a session

    1. On /admin/sessions, click New session.
    2. Enter a Title. If the session delivers a course, pick it under Delivers this course.
    3. Set Starts and Ends. The form suggests a date a week from today, 09:00–12:00.
    4. Choose the Type (In person, Online or Hybrid). Then fill in the Location, Room or Meeting link.
    5. Choose an Instructor or type an External instructor name.
    6. Set the Capacity (default 20) and Hours credited on attendance. If you leave the hours blank, the session length is used.
    7. Allow a waiting list when full is ticked to start with. Untick it if you do not want a waiting list.
    8. Click Save once. The form stays open after saving, and a second click creates a duplicate session.

    Employees book their own places on /sessions. Admins cannot add someone to a session. To change a session, open it from the list. The form under Edit session is lower down on the same page.

    To record attendance

    1. Open the session. Its Attendance register lists everyone holding a place. People on the waiting list are listed separately and cannot be marked.
    2. Tick Attended for each person who was there.
    3. Click Save attendance.

    Ticked people are recorded as Attended and unticked people as Absent. If the session is saved after its start time, it is set to Completed.

    If a course is linked, each person present has that course completed for them and receives its certificate and any badges earned. They also get the credited hours added to their record and a notification. Anyone present who was not yet enrolled in the course is enrolled at this point. Without a linked course, only attendance is recorded.

    Limits. Every save adds the credited hours again for everyone ticked, and notifies them again, so check the register before you save. Unticking someone later does not take back their hours, the completion or the certificate. If the linked course also has required online lessons, its progress is still worked out from those lessons, so it can stay In progress until the person has done them. A course that needs supervisor sign-off waits at 100% until the person's manager signs it off. Check the enrolment status before you rely on attendance for compliance.

    To cancel a session

    Type a Reason (sent to everyone registered). If you leave it empty, a standard message is sent. Click Cancel session, then Confirm. Everyone with a place or on the waiting list is notified. The session stays on record as Cancelled and cannot be reopened.

    Certificates

    /admin/certificates lists all certificates, 30 per page. You can search by certificate code, title or employee name, and filter by Status or Type (Course, Programme, Path). The Certificate ID link opens the public verification page, and PDF downloads the certificate. The verification page shows the holder's name as printed on the certificate. It answers up to 20 lookups a minute from each connection, then asks the visitor to wait a minute.

    To revoke a certificate

    1. In the Actions column, click the link labelled This certificate has been revoked. Despite the label, nothing is revoked yet. The link opens a reason box.
    2. Type a Reason of at least 3 characters.
    3. Click Confirm.

    The verification page then shows the certificate as revoked, without naming the holder, and the holder is notified with your reason. A revocation cannot be undone.

    Export to Excel downloads all certificates, whatever filters are set. You cannot issue a certificate by hand here. The platform issues them automatically and does not set expiry dates.

    A certificate is issued for every completed course that has evidence behind it: any T&C internal course, and an outside course once an administrator verifies the learner's proof, once attendance is marked at a classroom session for that course, or once a supervisor signs it off. An outside course that the learner simply marked complete, such as a video, earns no certificate. Courses completed before this rule came in were given their certificates once, automatically.

    The issuing company follows the person: it is the company of their Location, set on the Departments page (see above). People with no location receive T&C Garments certificates. A T&C certificate has the T&C mark, a red frame and a round seal. A T-CAP certificate has the T-GROUP logo, charcoal colours and a hexagonal seal. Both have a QR code at the top right that opens the public verification page. Signatures are printed names and titles, not handwritten signatures. By default, both companies' certificates name Ahmed Tolba, Chief Executive Officer, and T&C certificates also name Enis Dancir.     Link checking and withdrawal

    Once a day, up to 250 course links are checked. The check picks courses that have never been checked, are failing, or are past their review interval. Courses with no URL, withdrawn courses and freeCodeCamp links are never checked. Each failure is a strike, shown in a banner in the editor, and the engine stops recommending the course. After 3 failures in a row, the course is withdrawn from the Course Catalog and from recommendations. Its history is kept. A passing check clears the strikes.

    Restoring a withdrawn course. The banner says the next check puts the course back, but the automatic check skips withdrawn courses. Restore it by hand, as below.
    1. Fix the URL and click Save.
    2. In Course review, set Link working? and Still available? to Yes.
    3. Click Confirm.

    A manual review does not reset the strike count, so the next failed automatic check withdraws the course again straight away. A passing automatic check resets the count.

    Course review also records any manual check. Choosing No for Link working? stops recommendations. Choosing No for Still available? removes the course from the Course Catalog. Ticking Archived archives it. Every review clears Needs review, and the last five appear under Review history.

    Known limitations for administrators

    • Courses have no detail page. Course links on the Skills, Career and Team pages open the Course Catalog, searched for that course.
    • /register (Create your account) only lets someone claim an account that was added on the People page and that nobody has a password for yet. The person must never have signed in, and the email must match. An account that has been given a temporary password, by a spreadsheet import or with Change password, cannot be claimed. Its owner signs in with that temporary password instead. If such an account is still Invited, set it to Active first, because Invited accounts cannot sign in. /register cannot reactivate a deactivated account, and it is closed when single sign-on is set up.
    • Nothing AI-made is published automatically. AI-drafted courses open in the course editor as Draft. AI quiz questions arrive as Draft in their own question bank. Imported or discovered courses wait in the review queue (/admin/courses/review).
    • Import training history on /admin/enrollments adds one record at a time. It is not a file import.
    • Branding cannot be changed in the app. Default language is saved but not used anywhere. Course review interval (days) in Settings is not used either: each course has its own interval in the course editor, and new courses start at 180 days.
    • Reminder rules can only be switched on or off. Their wording, timing and channel cannot be edited, and new rules cannot be added in the app.
    • Reports have no filters, and the course completion and course effectiveness reports are currently the same file.
    • The department heatmap and the PDF's department analysis have no minimum group size.
    • The app has no Install button. Use your browser's own install option.
    • Notes in the Toolbox can be deleted but not created.
    • There is no screen for grading written answers.
    • Career ladders and the skill requirements for each job have no admin editor; they come from configuration. Only the critical-role flag is set in the app.
    • The app does not set expiry dates on certificates.
    • Challenges cannot be deleted, only paused. There are no individual leaderboards.

    This part is for the IT team. Most connections are switched on with server environment variables. The Integrations screen (/admin/api, Super Admin only, permission integrations.manage) lists them under Connections set on the server: Single sign-on (OpenID Connect), HR sync (SCIM 2.0) at /api/scim/v2 and SCORM on its own hostname, each marked On or Off.     Deployment at a glance

    • One Docker web service on Render (plan starter, Frankfurt, branch main), defined in render.yaml.
    • A 10 GB persistent disk at /data holds the SQLite database (/data/academy.db) and /data/storage (uploads, SCORM packages, backups). Without it, every deploy would erase the data.
    • Run exactly one instance. The disk attaches to one instance only, and rate limits and the scheduler live in memory.
    • Each start runs, in order: a pending restore (only if RESTORE_FROM is set), database migrations, a bootstrap (it seeds reference data into an empty database and creates the first administrator if asked), then the web server.
    • About 20 seconds after start, the course catalogue shipped with the app loads in the background, a few hundred rows at a time. The site works meanwhile.
    • Health check: /api/health. It returns 200 with status, database, courses (shows catalogue loading progress), latencyMs, uptimeSeconds and version. It returns 503 when the database cannot be reached.

    Single sign-on with Microsoft Entra ID

    The academy uses OpenID Connect (authorization code flow with PKCE). Signing in never creates an account. The person must already exist in the academy (from HR sync or HR's own setup) and must be Active.

    1. In the Microsoft Entra admin center, open App registrations and choose New registration. Pick single tenant (Accounts in this organizational directory only).
    2. Under Redirect URI, choose platform Web and enter https://tc-ai-academy.onrender.com/api/auth/oidc/callback. The general form is <APP_URL>/api/auth/oidc/callback.
    3. From Overview, copy the Application (client) ID and the Directory (tenant) ID.
    4. Under Certificates & secrets, create a client secret. Copy its Value (not the Secret ID) and note when it expires.
    5. Recommended: under Token configuration, add the optional claim email to the ID token. Under API permissions, grant admin consent for openid, email and profile.
    6. In Render, set OIDC_ISSUER=https://login.microsoftonline.com/<tenant-id>/v2.0, OIDC_CLIENT_ID, OIDC_CLIENT_SECRET and, optionally, OIDC_LABEL=Microsoft. Save and let the service restart.
    7. Check that Single sign-on (OpenID Connect) shows On on /admin/api. Check that the sign-in page shows Sign in with Microsoft. Without a label, the button reads Sign in with your company account.

    Webhooks

    Under Webhooks, enter an Address (must start with https://), choose the events under Send when (A course is completed and A certificate is issued, both ticked by default), then choose Add webhook. Copy the whsec_… secret, which is shown once. Each webhook shows On or Off, with Pause/Resume and Remove buttons. Removing a webhook also deletes its delivery history. The screen lists the last five deliveries as Delivered, Retrying or Failed, with the last error. There is no button to resend a failed delivery; use the API to catch up.

    { "event": "course.completed", "occurredAt": "2026-10-05T08:30:12.000Z",
  "data": { "enrollmentId": "…", "completedAt": "…",
    "employee": { "id": "…", "employeeCode": "…", "email": "…" },
    "course": { "id": "…", "slug": "…", "title": "…" } } }

    For certificate.issued, data holds certificateId, code, type, title, courseId, issuedAt, expiresAt (normally null, because the app does not set expiry dates) and employee. Each request carries the headers x-academy-event, x-academy-delivery and x-academy-signature: t=<unix>,v1=<hex>. The hex value is HMAC-SHA256 of t + "." + raw body, keyed with the whole secret, including the whsec_ prefix.

    import { createServer } from "node:http";
import { createHmac, timingSafeEqual } from "node:crypto";

const SECRET = process.env.ACADEMY_WEBHOOK_SECRET; // the whole whsec_… value
const seen = new Set(); // use a database table in production

createServer((req, res) => {
  let body = "";
  req.setEncoding("utf8");
  req.on("data", (chunk) => (body += chunk));
  req.on("end", () => {
    const m = /^t=(\d+),v1=([0-9a-f]{64})$/.exec(req.headers["x-academy-signature"] ?? "");
    if (!m) return res.writeHead(401).end();
    const expected = createHmac("sha256", SECRET).update(`${m[1]}.${body}`).digest("hex");
    const fresh = Math.abs(Date.now() / 1000 - Number(m[1])) < 300;
    if (!fresh || !timingSafeEqual(Buffer.from(expected), Buffer.from(m[2]))) {
      return res.writeHead(401).end();
    }
    const id = req.headers["x-academy-delivery"];
    if (!seen.has(id)) {
      seen.add(id);
      const { event, data } = JSON.parse(body);
      // handle the event here
    }
    res.writeHead(200).end();
  });
}).listen(8080);
    • The first attempt is sent straight away. Only a 2xx answer within 10 seconds counts as delivered. Redirects are not followed and count as failures.
    • A failed delivery waits 2, 4, 8, 16, then 32 minutes before the next try. Retries run at the next 15-minute scheduler check after that wait. After 6 attempts the delivery is marked Failed. Each attempt is signed afresh with the current time, so a 5-minute freshness window is safe.
    • A paused webhook keeps its queued deliveries until it is resumed. Events that happen while it is paused are not recorded for it.
    • Addresses that resolve to private, local or link-local networks are refused at every send.
    De-duplicate. Delivery is at least once, so the same event can arrive twice. Store x-academy-delivery and ignore any id you have already handled.

    SCORM on its own hostname

    A SCORM package is executable web content. Served from the academy's own address, it runs with the learner's signed-in session. On a separate hostname, it cannot read the academy's cookies, pages or storage. Do this before accepting packages from outside suppliers.

    1. Choose a second DNS name, for example scorm.example.com. A separate registered domain is the strongest choice.
    2. Add it to the Render service as a custom domain, point the DNS record at the service as Render instructs, and wait for the certificate.
    3. Set SCORM_CONTENT_ORIGIN=https://scorm.example.com and SCORM_TOKEN_SECRET to a long random value. APP_URL must also be set (it is on Render). Restart.
    4. Check that SCORM on its own hostname shows On. Then launch a SCORM lesson to test it.

    Once set, packages are served only there, through signed launch links valid for 8 hours for one learner and one package. Every other page on that hostname answers "Not found". In both modes, package files carry their own Content-Security-Policy that stops them loading anything from other hosts. With the variables unset, the original same-origin player is used.

    Backups and restore

    • Every day the scheduler writes a consistent copy of the database to /data/storage/backups (named academy-<date-time>.db). One copy is kept per day for 7 days, plus up to 10 copies from the latest day. To take a copy now, run npm run db:backup in the Render Shell.
    • These copies cover the database only. Uploads and SCORM files depend on Render's daily disk snapshots, which also protect against losing the whole disk.

    To restore on Render:

    1. In the Render Shell, list the copies with ls /data/storage/backups.
    2. Rehearse with npm run db:restore-check (newest copy) or npx tsx scripts/restore.mts --check <file>. Each runs an integrity check and compares row counts with the live database.
    3. Under Environment, set RESTORE_FROM=<backup file name> and restart.
    4. At start-up the copy is checked, the current database is kept as academy.db.before-restore-<time>, and the backup is put in place. Migrations then run as usual.
    5. Check /api/health and sign in. Then remove RESTORE_FROM. A .restored marker already stops the restore repeating.

    Anything saved after the backup was taken is lost. Outside Render, stop the application and run npm run db:restore -- <file> --yes.

    Wrong file name? If the named backup is missing or damaged, nothing is changed, but the service will not start until RESTORE_FROM is corrected or removed.
